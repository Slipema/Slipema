Lore & Story

The World of Curses

Cursed spirits are born from human negativity, evolving into threats like Mahito and Hanami.

Tengen's barrier stabilizes cursed energy, preventing chaos.

Jujutsu Sorcery

Jujutsu schools like Tokyo and Kyoto train sorcerers to combat curses.

Clan families like Gojo, Zenin, and Kamo play key roles in sorcery politics.

Key Events

The Night Parade of a Hundred Demons was a devastating attack orchestrated by Geto.

The Fearsome Womb arc marked Yuji's first mission with Megumi and Nobara.

The Cursed Objects

Sukuna’s fingers and the Death Painting Wombs are powerful relics driving the plot.
